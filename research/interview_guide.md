# Interview Guide

## Goal

Understand how people with atopic dermatitis experience and manage nighttime itching/scratching, what information they currently have or lack, and what they expect from a potential monitoring device.

## Interview Structure

### 1. Background & Recent Experience
- Relationship with atopic dermatitis
- When it started and recent condition
- Recent flare-up
- Current treatment/care

### 2. Nighttime Experience [CORE]
- Recent experience of nighttime itching/scratching
- When it starts during the night
- Whether scratching is conscious or unconscious
- How they know scratching happened
- Physical evidence or marks
- What they do afterward

### 3. Coping & Care
- Methods used to reduce nighttime itching/scratching
- What works / does not work
- Why they started or stopped using a method
- Established routines or habits

### 4. Impact on Sleep & Daily Life
- Sleep quality
- Next-day fatigue, concentration, work/school
- Mood
- Social interactions
- Clothing, photos, or other daily choices affected by skin condition

### 5. Triggers & Context
- Situations associated with worse nighttime symptoms
- Stress
- Weather / temperature / sweating
- Diet
- Sleep deprivation
- Environment

> Treat these as participant-perceived associations, not established causes.

### 6. Healthcare & Communication [CORE]
- How doctors ask about nighttime symptoms
- How participants describe nighttime experiences
- Difficulty recalling or explaining nighttime scratching
- Potential value of having more objective nighttime information

### 7. Recording & Information Needs [CORE]
- Whether they currently record symptoms
- Why they do / do not record
- What they would want to know the next morning
- Timing
- Duration
- Location
- Severity
- Changes over time
- What they would do with this information

### 8. Wearable Expectations
- Willingness to try nighttime monitoring
- Initial reaction to a wrist-worn device
- Comfort and sleep disruption
- Sweat / material / movement
- Charging and cleaning
- Privacy
- Most important requirements for long-term use

### 9. Closing
- Biggest nighttime challenge
- Anything important not covered

## Interview Principles

- Ask open-ended questions first.
- Ask about specific recent experiences rather than general opinions.
- Avoid suggesting answers unless the participant needs prompting.
- Separate participant perceptions from established medical facts.
- Do not frame monitoring as a treatment or promise behavior change.
- Pay particular attention to **nighttime awareness, information gaps, current coping behaviors, and willingness to use a wearable**.
